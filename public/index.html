How It Works

Three steps from intervention to permanent record

01
🔧
Professional Logs Event

An authorized garage, inspection center, or OEM logs every intervention — repairs, maintenance, accidents, or part replacements — into cartrace.

02
⛓️
Blockchain Secures Data

Each record is signed with the professional's cryptographic wallet and permanently written to the blockchain. It cannot be edited or deleted by anyone.

03
🔍
Buyer Verifies History

Any buyer can search a VIN for free with no account needed, and instantly see the vehicle's complete, verified history — including a trust score.
